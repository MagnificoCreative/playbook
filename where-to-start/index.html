The short version

You don’t fix operational data by buying a better dashboard. You fix it by listening to your people, following your data back to its source, agreeing on what matters, designing around real decisions, and testing before you build.

Advanced capabilities come last, because they depend on everything before them.

Start this month

You can start this month, without new technology:

Talk across functions

Have a handful of honest conversations about how people really work with operational data.

Trace one metric

Pick a number your leadership relies on and follow it from where it’s born to where it’s used.

Sketch one decision

Map how one call really gets made, from the first signal to the action that resolves it.

Each of those will tell you more about your real problem than another dashboard would.
