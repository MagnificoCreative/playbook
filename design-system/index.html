    01

    Principles

    Five ideas that every page, component and animation in the playbook answers to.

    Calm over clever

    Lots of space, few elements, one idea at a time. If something feels busy, remove a part before adding a fix.

    Pink is the signal

    Magnifico pink marks the one thing that matters: the action, the current chapter, the ball. Never use it as decoration.

    One scale, few sizes

    Type uses a short, shared scale. If two sizes look almost the same, they should be the same.

    Lines tell the story

    Diagrams and heroes speak through hairlines and motion, not icons or illustration.

    Readable first

    Text always wins over art. Backgrounds move behind a veil, and every animation has a still version.

    Colour rules

    Do
    • Use pink for the current chapter number and the ball; fill buttons with --pink-action, which keeps white labels readable.
    • Set hierarchy in diagrams with opacity of one line colour, not new hues.
    • Use --text-muted for supporting copy on the dark teal.
    Don't
    • Introduce new colours for charts or states.
    • Put --text-muted grey on the light canvas, or white text on brand pink #ff0074; both fail contrast.
    • Use pink for large areas or as a background behind text.

    Type rules

    Do
    • Use Syne for anything that reads as a heading, including card labels and diagram titles.
    • Keep body copy at 16px, 300 weight, line height around 1.5, and 45–70 characters per line.
    • Use sentence case for eyebrows and headings inside sections; title case only for chapter and hero titles.
    • Break short lines by hand where it improves the rag (and drop the break on phones).
    Don't
    • Set text smaller than 12px, or between steps (13, 15, 17, 18…).
    • Use Syne for paragraphs, or Inter for headlines.
    • Bold body text for emphasis; use <strong> at 500 weight sparingly.

    06

    Motion

    Smooth, calm and a little playful. Motion explains an idea or rewards scrolling; it never decorates. Full catalogue in the animation library.

    Ease out · --ease-out cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)

    The default for every interface transition: fast start, long gentle settle.

    Pink: ease out. Grey: linear, kept only for steady flows such as the drifting footer texture.

    Durations

    KindDurationExamples
    Hover and state0.2–0.3sButton colour, row hover, logo pink deepening, arrow nudge
    Layout0.45–0.55sMenu open and collapse, caret turn, top-bar title sliding
    Reveal0.9s, staggered 0.04sHeadings and text rising 16px into place
    ScrubbedTied to scroll, 0.6–0.8 smoothingPinned diagrams, gathering bullet dots
    Ambient loops7–120sHero scenes, card scenes, footer drift

    Motion rules

    Do
    • Keep the pink ball at the centre of heroes; it breathes when something reaches it.
    • Leave a fading trail behind moving lines (10 echoes, opacity .42 × (1 − j/11)²).
    • Pin a section below the top bar with the shared gap (P.pinTop()), except How to apply.
    • Clamp scroll animations near the end of a page so they always finish.
    • Pause scenes off screen and show a still frame under reduced motion.
    Don't
    • Loop with a visible jump; fade out before resetting.
    • Move lines in lockstep; give each its own timing.
    • Set stroke-opacity in CSS on animated lines; it overrides the animation.
    • Let motion sit behind text without a veil.

    08

    Accessibility

    Built in, not added later. Check these before shipping a change.

    Contrast

    Body text and button labels meet AA on their backgrounds (see Colour). Muted grey is for the dark teal only. Pink text is for short labels and numbers, never paragraphs.

    Reduced motion

    Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion: heroes and cards show a still frame, scrubbed diagrams show their end state, the footer stops drifting.

    Decorative art

    Hero, card and background art is aria-hidden="true". Diagrams that carry meaning get role="img" and a full aria-label.

    Keyboard and structure

    A skip link to the content, one h1 per page, buttons with labels that change with state ("Collapse contents" / "Expand contents"), Escape closes the drawer.

    Targets

    Menu rows, the caret and the collapsed numbers are 36px; buttons are 44px tall.

    Links out

    The assessment and wearemagnifico.com open in a new tab with rel="noopener".
