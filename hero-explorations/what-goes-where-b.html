Step 5 · What goes where · Option B

Layers, refined

The three layers, each in its tier's color, with no pink dot. They start as outlines, and the tier being presented turns solid as you scroll. Once all three have been shown, they draw together into a tighter stack that overlaps a little. The three versions differ in what stays solid and how the overlap reads.

B1 · One at a time B2 · Builds up B3 · See-through

Option B1

One at a time

Exactly as you described. Only the tier being presented is solid: always visible goes solid, then back to an outline as on demand goes solid, then investigation. At the end, all three return to outlines and draw together, overlapping slightly.

Option B2

Builds up

Each tier stays solid once it has been presented, so the stack fills in from the top down. When they draw together, the solid layers overlap like stacked cards, with always visible on top.

Option B3

See-through

Like B2, but the fills are slightly transparent. When the layers draw together, you can see each one through the one above it, so the overlap reads as layering rather than covering.
