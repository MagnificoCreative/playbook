Step 2 · Infographic options

Five roles, one truth

Three ways to show that every role works from the same truth, at the depth its decisions need. Each one drops the dashboard and the table: the roles are a plain menu, the copy keeps need and friction, and the illustration carries the depth. Choose a role to see it move.

A · One tree, five depths B · Friction to flow C · The lens

Option A

One tree, five depths

The pink ball is the shared truth, and everything below it is the same data broken down further. Each role lights up only the levels it reads, and a ring drops down one path to show where its attention goes. When you switch roles, the tree shakes loose for a moment (the friction) and then settles back into place.

Option B

Friction to flow

Five lines, one for each role, run toward the pink ball. Each one starts out tangled, which is that role's friction. The role you choose straightens out, reaches the truth, and then drops below it only as deep as its decisions need. A signal keeps running down the line you've chosen.

Option C

The lens

One field of data, nine groups of assets, and one lens. The pink ball is the number everyone ends up watching. The executive sees the whole field folded into nine summaries. Each role after that zooms in further, until the engineer is looking at a single asset and the history behind it. It's the same data the whole way down.
