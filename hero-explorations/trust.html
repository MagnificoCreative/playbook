Where trust is lost

Four takes on the Source → Prepare → Reconcile → Present → Act flow. Each one is pinned and scrubbed by scroll, so the story plays as you read and rewinds when you scroll back. Each one also has one small thing to poke at once it lands.

Option A · Horizontal

Copy of a copy

The line forks at every handoff. One number becomes two, then three, then five, then seven. When it reaches Act, the pink drains out and the copies shuffle like a shell game, so you can't tell which is the original.

Poke it: hover any ball at the end to see its value and where it was copied. Only one of them is the source.

Option B · Horizontal chart

The slope

It reads like a real chart: trust on the y axis, the process on the x axis. The line steps down at each handoff with a short, deadpan reason for each drop. A counter ticks down from 100% as you scroll, and the gap to where it started fills in.

Poke it: move across the chart to read the trust left at any point.

Option C · Horizontal

Broken telephone

The number travels as a signal. It's a clean, still line at Source. Every handoff adds noise and the line starts to wobble. By Reconcile it has split into strands that don't quite agree, and by Act it's a live, nervous scribble.

Poke it: hover the line. Wherever you look, the noise drops away and you see the clean signal it started as.

Option D · Vertical

Chipped away

The number drops down the process wearing a ring of trust. At each stage a piece of the ring breaks off and hops to the side, where it's labeled with what it cost. It reaches Act with a thin sliver left. This one suits narrow screens best.

Poke it: at the end, tap the ball. The pieces fly back and the ring closes, which is what a single source of truth does.
