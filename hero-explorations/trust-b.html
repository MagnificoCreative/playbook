Where trust is lost

Operational information loses trust on its way from source to decision. It’s born in sensors, records and systems. It’s exported and reshaped in spreadsheets. It’s reconciled by hand before meetings. It’s presented in dashboards built by different teams. By the time it reaches a decision, nobody is sure which version to believe.
