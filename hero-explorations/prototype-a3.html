Step 6 · Option A3, refined

From prototype to platform

A3 with your changes. The platform and its predictive layer are now centered as one group on the dotted line, so the line meets the middle of the pair. The Blueprint and Delivered platform labels share one baseline. The pink line and dot inside the layer are gone. Instead, the layer holds a small pattern of linked diamonds that rise from the platform's tiles, in four arrangements.

1 · Lattice 2 · Mesh 3 · Cells 4 · Nested

Pattern 1

Lattice

Nine small diamonds, one above each platform tile, each joined to its tile by a faint dotted post. They link to their neighbors along the plane, so the layer reads as an ordered lattice of data.

1 · Lattice

Pattern 2

Mesh

The same nine diamonds, plus four more sitting between them. The diagonal links close up into diamond-shaped cells, a little denser and more interconnected.

2 · Mesh

Pattern 3

Cells

The layer is divided into a quiet grid of diamond cells. A connected run of them fills in lightly, with small diamonds at the corners they share, like a pattern found in the data.

3 · Cells

Pattern 4

Nested

Three diamonds sit one inside the other, joined corner to corner, with small diamonds where the lines meet. It's the most composed and symmetrical of the four.

4 · Nested
