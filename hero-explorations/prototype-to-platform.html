Step 6 · Infographic options

From prototype to platform

Six takes on the path from prototype to platform, all in the refined line language: 1px paths, 0.5px hairlines, and a single pink dot carrying the story. The dot laps prototype, test and refine twice, hands off, breaks into an ordered blueprint, and becomes the delivered platform, with the predictive layer sitting on top in perspective. Option A is the original, refined. Each one is pinned and scrubbed by scroll.

Option A

The original, refined

Left to right, as before. The loop starts dotted and goes solid as the pink dot laps it twice, lighting prototype, test and refine as it passes each one. The dot follows the hand-off line into the blueprint and breaks into an ordered grid of dots, a few of them pink. One of those leaves, carries on, and spreads into the platform, drawn in perspective. The predictive layer then rises to sit on top of it.

Option C

The spiral

Each lap is tighter than the last, so the loop becomes a spiral that converges. That's the point of refining: every round narrows the design until it holds. The dot leaves from the tight center, and the rest follows A.

Option D

Sketch to clean

The first lap is drawn as a rough, wobbly sketch, like a first prototype. The second lap is a clean circle over the top of it. It shows "test and refine" as a change in quality, not just repetition. The rest follows A.

Option E

Built in layers

Every stage is a plane in one stack, building upward. The loop runs on the prototype plane at the bottom. The dot rises to the blueprint plane and becomes an ordered grid, rises again to become the platform, and the predictive layer lands on top in pink. It's the same visual language as the layers in step 5.

Option F

The quiet rail

The most minimal option. A single line with a small orbit at the start, where the dot circles twice, then a tiny dot grid, a small platform and a pink layer along the line. It takes the least room and suits a section where the picture supports the copy rather than leading it.
