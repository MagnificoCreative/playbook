1. Map

    Map who to talk to.

    List every function that creates, interprets or acts on operational data: operations leadership, production, maintenance and reliability, integrity, health and safety, projects, and technology. Include frontline people as well as managers, and always include IT.

  2. Ask

    Ask about work, not wish lists.

    Don’t ask what dashboard people want. Ask them to walk you through a recent decision: which tools they opened, which numbers they checked by hand, where they keep their own records, and why.

  3. Evidence

    Treat workarounds as evidence.

    The spreadsheet someone trusts more than the official system is the most useful thing you’ll find. Note every one.

  4. Patterns

    Look for the pattern behind the symptoms.

    Ask what in your culture, incentives or habits lets fragmentation grow. That is what will have to change, and no tool will change it for you.
