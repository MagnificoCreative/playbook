Magnifico playbook

Animation library

Every animation made for From Data Sprawl to a Single Source of Truth: what’s on the pages now, what’s parked for later and what’s been retired. Nothing gets deleted; it gets listed here.

Hero and card scenes play live below. Section animations open on their page.

Adding an animation

  1. Follow the visual rules in hero-explorations/ANIMATION-RULES.md: one pink ball, slate or teal lines, opacity for hierarchy, calm motion.
  2. Explore options in hero-explorations/, one HTML page per idea.
  3. Move the pick into the playbook: hero and card scenes go in assets/motion/; section animations go in tools/build/scripts.js, under the chapter’s // ==== key ==== block.
  4. Add an entry to CATALOG at the bottom of this page with its status, where it’s used and its source.
  5. When something comes off a page, don’t delete it. Set its status to retired or parked, note the date and what replaced it, and keep the code.
